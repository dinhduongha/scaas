## Checked and sound

**Statement binding (PQZ, Zether, relayer / front-runner swap)**
- PQZ `_header` (PqzCore.sol:875-880) binds opTag, psid, chainid, token, registry, from, to / recipientsHash, beneficiary, fee (txFee + M·d, so d is fixed given ben and the immutable confidentialFee), amount, recipient (op 7: M at word 320), sendCounter, p, validUntilBlock, auditorOn, shieldIn, chainUid, binding, salt and memoHash. This matches the §7.7 table apart from row 544 (C0-C14).
- Keys pk_f / pk_t / pk_j / pk_a come from registry pointers. T is rebuilt from state. `accHint` is used only when `keccak(hint) == accHash` (PqzCore.sol:770). The `beneficiary` argument must equal the payload word (`_ben`, PqzLayer.sol:298-303).
- authDigest is `keccak("SCAAS-PQZ-AUTH-V1" ‖ keccak(stmt))`, checked via `requireAuthorized(o.from, PURPOSE_ZETHER_AUTH)` before the precompile (PqzCore.sol:808-826). PQZ is the only user of that purpose.
- D15 / W-A is complete for PQZ:
  - transfers: `_bindMode` (PqzCore.sol:216-218), direct `keccak(TAG_DIRECT_MODE, mode)`, relayed `keccak(b, mode)`;
  - burn: direct 0, relayed `b`;
  - unwrap: direct `unwrapBinding(u, minOut)`, relayed `keccak(b, w)` (UnderlyingPqzToken.sol:94-98).
  - These are pairwise distinct, so no proof moves between paths, modes, or `mtxUnshield` / `mtxUnshieldUnwrap`.
- Envelope bindings (PqzLayer.sol:310-323; ZetherLayer.sol:262-295) use distinct address-path and UA-path tags. They cover chainid, CHAIN_UID, token, from, batch, nonce, validUntil, mode, the canonical relayer, fee and the canonical feeRecipient. `data` is covered by the envelope signature.
- `shieldIn` payer: relayed, it is `_msgSender()` / the ticket subject, which the binding names via `m.from`. Direct, it is `msg.sender`, so a front-runner can only pay the victim's top-up (PqzLayer.sol:270-276).
- Zether: the verifier absorbs the whole statement before the Σ challenge (ZetherVerifier `_ctx`, :406-422). The multi mode is in opTag (ZetherLib.sol:582-585). `FEE_AT` 288 and `AMOUNT_AT` 352 match `_header`. The rollOverSigned / setRollOverMode digests bind chainid, chainUid, token, ua, accSeq or modeNonce, and validUntilBlock (ZetherLib.sol:362-401). The only gaps are C0-C7 and the documented unwrap residual.

**Race-freedom of debits**
- A rollover between proving and inclusion leaves T byte-identical mod q in both directions. Up case: `acc′ + Σ[r+n, p) = acc + Σ[r, p)`. Down case: `acc′ − Σ[p, r+n)`. `unitsOf(T)` is invariant (PqzCore.sol:741-760; PqzStateLib.sol:73-107).
- Windows never read a deleted slot (cleared ≤ floor ≤ p). An un-rolled entry is counted exactly once.
- A TRIVIAL merge touches only a current-block tail at index ≥ max(floor, rolled) (PqzCore.sol:339-355). The same-block residual is documented.
- `sendCounter` and authDigest stop replays.
- The documented residuals (capped p, same-block merge, Zether PUBLIC-mode accSeq bump with its OWNER_ONLY mitigation, zether.md:343-351) hold as stated. The one exception is the post-proof-entry case (C0-C5).

**Balance conservation**
- PQZ: `_debit` does `totalShielded += shieldIn − fee − amount` with checked math (PqzCore.sol:840). `_pay` moves exactly `shieldIn` in, then `fee − legs·d` to ben, then d to each leg, then the amount out, or the pool is burnt before the leg payout on an unwrap (PqzLayer.sol:217-224, 286-294; UnderlyingPqzToken.sol:56-78).
- SHIELD_POOL is reachable only via `_poolMove`: `_update`, `_uaMove`, `_checkTo` and the fee paths refuse it (ERC20MetaTx.sol:281, 407, 436, 454, 477). So `balanceOf(SHIELD_POOL) == totalShielded·scale`.
- Zether shield, unshield, single and multi match the pool moves (ZetherLib.sol:209-217, 310-328, 657-673; ZetherLayer.sol:127-179, 234-252). E5 conserves value without mod-r wrap (cap 2^54, v_j < 2^64, M ≤ 16, fee < 2^64).
- K_MAX: a rollover clamps at K_MAX − 1, and a refresh at p = rolled always fits, so a full acc never locks.

**Privacy (events, storage, decoys, auditor modes, timing)**
- Every PQZ op 7 leg is credited, laid out and paid the same way: the same decoyFee goes to every leg, the real one included (`_creditLegs`, `_bundleMulti`, `_pay`). Queue exits are prefix-only. Events carry only public fields.
- The PQZ auditor copies after R0-1 use windows `w = j mod S_AUD` (`_groups`, `_groupOf`, disclosure kind word). F-4 (`_noAuditorParty`) applies on op 1 and op 7. Key uniqueness (UAKeyInUse) prevents twin keys.
- Burn and refresh carry salt 0 with fresh randomness. ZeroU is refused on every u / u_g / u′.
- The auditor modes are enforced by length or point on both layers. The OPTIONAL opt-out is visible, as documented.
- Classical Zether without the auditor: telling legs apart is a DDH problem. The auditor leak is C0-C1. SENDER_S opening every leg is documented (Z3; SENDER_CP is the per-leg alternative).

**Disclosure**
- PQZ: the statement binds tag, psid, chainid, chainUid, token, registry, entryRef, amount < 2^64 and the kind word with the window. u_{g(j)} and v_j / v_{a,j} come from the stored blob, and pk from the immutable registry record (PqzCore.sol:606-666). One proof opens one leg and one amount. `_bundleShape` is unique at all four sets for M = 1..8 with the auditor on and off.
- Zether: SENDER_S and commit-reveal are sound, and a HASH record can never be misparsed (parity). The Chaum–Pedersen kinds RECEIVER_X / AUDITOR / SENDER_CP are not sound under collusion; see C0-C2.

**Domain separation and replay**
- `uaMessage = keccak(MSG_TAG ‖ TAG(kind) ‖ purpose ‖ digest)` (UAKinds.sol:209-211). Native ids use EIP-712 with generation, chainId, registry and chainUid (UAKinds.sol:226-240).
- Every caller digest carries chain and contract (PQZ statement, metatx / trade EIP-712, metatx-cancel, Zether owner digests, `popDigest`). The msig member digest binds the structure UA.
- entryRef binds chainid, chainUid, token, from and counter. psid is pinned via `requirePqz` plus the self-test (W-B).
- Cross-generation fallbacks (`recordOf`, `keyOwner`, `requireAuthorized`) are sound, because the token's registry is immutable and part of the header.
- The Zether UA Schnorr tag is separate from `schnorr-bls12381`, and both kinds share the keyHash group `bls12381-g1`.

**Zether protocol on BLS12-381**
- E1-E8 for single, multi and burn match the statement indices. Y_f ≠ O, Y_t ≠ O, Y_f ≠ Y_t and D_out ≠ O are checked.
- The transcript absorbs statement → Σ commitments (V included) → c. The Bulletproof challenges y, z, x, w, u_j chain on the same state, with length-prefixed absorbs, scalars < r and zero challenges refused.
- The Bulletproof verifier algebra is correct: δ(y,z), BP-1, the BP-2 coefficients and the s-vector order.
- Every point passes the G1MSM 0x0c subgroup check, and G1ADD is applied only to points already checked. The RFC 9380 generators are correct.

**PQ signature choices**
- ML-DSA-44/65 parameters and Verify_internal match FIPS 204 (ExpandA, SampleInBall, HintBitUnpack, UseHint, μ with the empty context). FN-DSA-512 matches Falcon round 3 (header bytes, comp_decode, HashToPoint, norm bound 34,034,726). SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s matches FIPS 205 (H_msg, digest split, ADRS, FORS, WOTS+, 7,856 B).
- ML-DSA / SLH-DSA sign `pq_msg = keccak(TAG ‖ ua_msg)` with the empty FIPS context; Falcon signs `ua_msg`.
- The ML-DSA expanded-key cache is filled only from the authenticated pk under `uaOf(kind, pk)`.
- The PQZ key R_REG runs over `uaMessage(0x0142, POP, popDigest(ua, h))`, the key is written once, and keyOwner is unique, so a front-run registration gives an identical record.

**PQZH proposal (A) against the owner rules**
- Ciphertexts stay in state. The regime is the stricter EIP-7976, with one rule for every chain. Both relay paths are measured. Fee 0 is valid. HASH memos fit the uaExecute headroom.
- Gaps: the holder-kind and fee-UA coverage (C0-C3, C0-C4, C0-C11) and the code-level enforcement (C0-C6).

**Gap-round items (all five critic gaps were covered)**
