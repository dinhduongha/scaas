## Checked and sound

- **Statement bytes, every op.**
  - Header (`PqzCore.sol:875-880`): the 20 words follow the `pqz-stmt` H_* order (`stmt/src/lib.rs:62-83`) and spec §7.7. The salt is `uint256(uint128(salt))`.
  - Op 1: body `pk_f‖pk_t‖[pk_a]‖T‖u‖v_t‖v_f‖[v_a]`; amount and recipient are 0.
  - Op 2 (burn / refresh): `pk_f‖T‖u'‖v_f`; to, auditorOn and salt are 0, and a refresh also has amount and recipient 0.
  - Op 7: word 192 = keccak of the legs, word 320 = M, G = ⌈M/S_AUD⌉ with the auditor and ⌈M/4⌉ without, M = 8 refused under PQZH.
  - Op 3 (`UAPqzVerifier._call`): `u8(3)‖be32(96+PK)‖…`, key length and canonical form checked.
  - Ops 4 / 5 (disclosure): 9 words, with `kind + 256·(j mod S_AUD)` for AUDITOR only.
  - Lengths match `STMT_*` and `multiLen` at n2304 (93,312 / 74,752 / 56,192 / 37,280). Op tags and the ERC-7201 slot were recomputed and match (two reviewers).
  - `_bundleShape` decodes uniquely at every set.
- **Precompile call.** `s.pqz` is pinned to `PqzSafety.PQZ`, the psid is restricted, and the self-test runs at init (`PqzStateLib.sol:42-44`, the W-B fix is still in place). The input is `u8(op)‖be32(stLen)‖stmt‖proof`, with `gas()` forwarded. Only a successful call returning exactly 32 bytes equal to 1 is accepted (`PqzCore.sol:681-687`, `UAPqzVerifier.sol:66-75`). authDigest is `keccak("SCAAS-PQZ-AUTH-V1"‖keccak(stmt))` under `PURPOSE_ZETHER_AUTH`, which nothing else uses, and it is checked before the precompile runs. For views, see C2-C4.
- **State machine.**
  - `cleared ≤ floor ≤ rolled ≤ pendingCount` is kept by `_debit` (`PqzCore.sol:832-842`) and `rollOver` (`PqzStateLib.sol:73-107`). Deletions are at most 32 per debit and at most n per rollover.
  - An un-roll subtracts exactly the entries already in acc. A rollover leaves both T and the unit count of an in-flight debit unchanged.
  - A refresh at p = rolled always works (units ≤ K_MAX), so there is no permanent lock.
  - The TRIVIAL merge touches only a same-block tail at index ≥ max(floor, rolled), with the sum < 2^64.
  - `prepareAcc` is idempotent and cannot overwrite a materialised acc. A rollover requires PREP, and a debit keeps PREP and clears MAT.
  - accHint is used only under MAT, at length CTP, with keccak equal to accHash.
  - `sendCounter` is incremented on every debit, and entryRef is unique per debit. For the rollover residual, see C2-C6.
- **P46 packing and lane bounds.** At B = 42 / 46 / 50 / 54 (all even, GROUP = B/2 bytes), packing and expansion are exact inverses. The in-place descending expand never overwrites an unread group. The EO accumulators keep a B-bit carry gap per field. `(ROLL_MAX + 1)·q < 2^64` holds at 97 / 81 / 65 / 33 times q, and a debit window is at most 18 terms. The subtractions never borrow. `PQZ_ACC_WORDS` = ⌈CTP/32⌉ = 339 / 417 / 504 / 598. uint16 blob offsets hold at the worst case, n2048 op 7 M = 8 with the auditor (44,708 B).
- **Ciphertexts in state.** Every debit writes an SSTORE2 op blob, including burns. Pending slots point into blobs, acc is either a blob reference or packed slots, and HASH-mode memos are stored. `pqzAcc`, `pqzPendingCt`, `pqzEntryBlob` and `transferEntry` restore everything from state. The non-zero slot invariant holds; see C2-C12 for its stated reason.
- **SHIELD_POOL and fees.**
  - Every debit does `totalShielded += shieldIn − fee − amount` with checked arithmetic (`PqzCore.sol:840`), and `_pay` mirrors it exactly: shieldIn in, txFee = fee − M·d to the beneficiary, d to each leg. Unshield moves `amount·scale` out; unwrap burns it from the pool.
  - Only `_poolMove` can touch SHIELD_POOL: `_update` (`ERC20MetaTx.sol:281`) and `_uaMove` (`:477`) refuse it.
  - Fees: d ∈ [minDecoyFee, 2^64) and fee < 2^64. A fee of 0 is valid. init bounds the cap and the fees, and `maxShielded·shieldScale` cannot overflow.
  - Recipients 0, either pool, and `this` are refused. The SOL-01 / W-S unwrap rounding dust was recorded before and is not re-reported.
- **Auditor.** auditorOn is fixed by the payload length and checked against NONE / FULL. AuditorAsParty is checked for from / to on op 1 and from / every leg on op 7, on every path and in the views (`PqzCore.sol:222-235`, `:434-449`). Keys are unique per registry (UAKeyInUse). In FULL mode the auditor can still burn its own funds.
- **Disclosure views.** The 9 bound words, pk taken from the registry, and u / v taken from the stored blob (`_legOf`, `disclosureBuffer`). Kind > 2, amount ≥ 2^64, a missing leg and AUDITOR without copies all revert, and the view turns those into `false`. For out-of-gas and short payloads, see C2-C4 and C2-C7.
- **ROLL_MAX gas.** The measurement (`PqzStateGas.t.sol:202-358`) uses a prepared, materialised account with PRE = 16 and cold, distinct blobs, on both `mtxExecute` and `uaExecute`. The n2816 value is pinned at 32 on the costlier path (c7e784a9). The logs were read, not re-run.
- **Reentrancy and external calls.** Every entry point is `nonReentrant` with the transient ticket. Registry and precompile calls are staticcalls. The underlying wrap measures the received delta (`UnderlyingLib.sol:156-177`). `payOutTo` updates the reserve, then makes one external call and checks the exact balance drop. A failed self-call reverts the whole envelope, so no nonce is burnt.
- **Access control and initialisation.** `initialize` is factory-only and runs once, and the implementation is locked in its constructor (`MetaTokenBase.sol:56-82`). The registry's chainUid is checked. The verifier is the release immutable `PQZ_VERIFIER`, not chosen by the creator. Library state functions can only be reached by DELEGATECALL. The committed `PqzSet.sol` is byte-identical to `pqz-core/params/sets/n2304-kmax4096/PqzSet.sol`. At n2816, HAS_PQZH is false and PQZH_ID is 0.
- **Relayability and bindings.** Every state-changing PQZ `mtx*` is in `mtxSelectors` with the ABSOLUTE fee class and correct static shapes (68 / 100 bytes), and none can be batched. All are UA-allowed except `mtxWrapShield`, by design (C2-C9). Bindings: direct transfers use `keccak(TAG_DIRECT_MODE, mode)`, relayed ones `keccak(envelope, mode)`. Burn is 0 direct and the envelope binding relayed. Unwrap is `unwrapBinding` direct and `keccak(B, w)` relayed. The address and UA tags differ. So no proof crosses modes, paths or entry points, and the W-A / T18 / D15 fixes are complete.
- **Previous-round fixes still present.** W-A, W-B, the CHAIN-01 zero-u carry-over (`PqzCore.sol:256`, `:460-462`, `:586`) and the `_parseMulti` part of W-I. The view part of W-I is still open (C2-C7).
