## Checked and sound

- **The verifier checks every equation, over the full transcript.**
  - The Σ equations match zether.md §8.1, §8.2 and §11 term by term: E1–E8 for single transfers, E1/E2/E3_j/E4(Σz_vj)/E5/E6_j/E7_j/E8 for multi, and E1/E5(amount+fee)/E8 for burn (ZetherVerifier.sol:443-595). The responses are shared where they must be (z_x, z_s, z_v, z_b).
  - The statement layouts match the builders' point order (ZetherLib.sol:497-500, 557-559, 577-594). `FEE_AT 288` and `AMOUNT_AT 352` are header words 9 and 11.
  - The 15-word header binds op, psid, chainid, chainUid, token, tag, binding, sendCounter, accSeq, fee, beneficiary, amount, recipient, salt and memoHash (:567-574).
  - `_ctx` absorbs the whole statement, then every Σ commitment including V_v, V_b and V_vj, then derives c (:412-423). The Bulletproofs continue the same transcript, the framing is length-prefixed (ZetherTranscript.sol:13-23), every challenge must be non-zero and every scalar < r.
  - The Bulletproof algebra is standard: δ, BP-1, the BP-2 G_i/H_i/U coefficients, the u²/u⁻² weights and the s-vector order (:217-306).
  - The only payload word outside the statement is decoyFee, and it is fixed by `fee = innerFee(beneficiary) + M·d` (ZetherLib.sol:522-526).
- **Ring and decoys cannot be abused.**
  - 1 ≤ M ≤ 16 in both the library and the verifier. Recipients are pairwise distinct, none is the sender, all are registered as kind 0x0141 (ZetherLib.sol:462-471, 597-610).
  - The verifier rejects `Y_tj = O`, `Y_tj = Y_f`, `D_out = O` and `Y_f = O` (ZetherVerifier.sol:450-451, 497, 556). The single format rejects a self-transfer (:490).
  - The decoy fee lies in [minDecoyFee, 2^64), and `txFee + M·d < 2^64`. Rogue related keys are blocked by the registry's proof of possession.
  - The only gap is C3-C1 (shared auditor mask).
- **Epochs and roll-over cannot be raced into a double spend or a lock.**
  - `_checkSeqs` pins accSeq and sendCounter before verification (:612-618). Every debit bumps sendCounter (:662-665), and only a roll-over bumps accSeq (:684-695). Credits touch only pending, so in-flight proofs survive them.
  - `rollOverSigned` requires `lastRolled < expected ≤ pendingSeq` and pins accSeq, so each signature works once. `setRollOverMode` pins modeNonce. Both digests carry chainid, chainUid, token and the deadline (:362-401).
  - OWNER_ONLY accounts cannot be locked, because the owner can always roll over by signature. The registry is append-only. Griefing in PUBLIC mode is an accepted spec item (zether.md:343-349).
- **Shield, unshield and fees conserve value.**
  - Shield: `totalShielded += amount`, and the pool receives `amount·scale`.
  - Transfer: the account is debited `C_deb + [fee]G`, `totalShielded −= txFee + M·d`, and the pool pays exactly `Paid` (ZetherLib.sol:267, 657-659; ZetherLayer.sol:241-251).
  - Unshield: `amount + fee` is removed on both sides (ZetherLib.sol:322-325; ZetherLayer.sol:176-178).
  - Wrap-shield mints only once and enforces `minted % scale == 0`. Unshield-unwrap burns `amount·scale` and pays `⌊wrapped·den/num⌋` (UnderlyingZetherToken.sol:57-98; UnderlyingLib._quote).
  - SHIELD_POOL can be moved only through `_poolMove` (ERC20MetaTx.sol:278-289, 476-496). Amounts cannot wrap mod r: `b' + Σv + fee < 18·2^64`. `cap·scale` cannot overflow (ZetherLib.sol:178-185).
- **Ciphertexts stay in state.** acc and pending (C, D) are stored as points (ZetherLib.sol:23-34). The single and BUNDLE forms store R in full. HASH and NONE affect disclosure records only and follow the owner's design (see Refuted).
- **Relayability and binding of the transfer mode.**
  - All Zether mtx* ops are on the mtx allowlist and the UA allowlist (ZetherLayer.sol:280-287; the tokens' `mtxSelectors` / `_uaAllowed`). `mtxWrapShield` is left off the UA path by design. A fee of 0 is accepted.
  - The multi mode is in opTag (ZetherLib.sol:585-587; verifier :372-374). COMPACT tokens accept HASH only.
  - DIRECT and RELAYED tags are distinct, and so are the address-envelope and UA-envelope tags. Both bindings are keccak outputs, so never 0, and the ticket binding is read only during a live self-call (MetaTxBase.sol:213-216, 276-279).
  - A direct unwrap binds `u` and `minOut`. The remaining doc gap is C3-C3.
- **Reentrancy and access control.**
  - Every entry point is nonReentrant, and `_selfCall` fails closed if its target is unguarded (MetaTxBase.sol:56-67, 219-226). The single external payout in unshield-unwrap comes after the debit and the pool burn. Verifier and registry calls are view.
  - `initialize` is factory-only and runs once (MetaTokenBase.sol:70-82). `auth` and `accHint` must be empty (ZetherLayer.sol:227-230). Owner ops need a registered Zether UA plus `requireAuthorized` under the zether-owner purpose.
- **Points, precompiles and hash-to-curve.**
  - Every external point passes through G1MSM 0x0c (subgroup check) before any G1ADD state update. Every BLS precompile call checks the exact return size (ZetherCurves.sol:177-239, Bls12381.sol:69-109).
  - The auditor key is validated at init. RFC 9380 expand_message_xmd and map×2 + G1ADD are correct (HashToCurve.sol:49-125).
  - Gap round: `NEG_G2_GENERATOR`, the pairing order in `verifyMinSig` (sig, −G2, H(m), pk), `requireInG2Subgroup` (G2MSM) and the refusal of an identity pk or sig (CurveSigs.sol:63) are correct.
- **Calldata bounds, assembly and storage.**
  - `singleFrom`, `multiFrom` and `unshieldFrom`, and the verifier's statement and proof lengths, are all checked before any slice. `_copy`, `_slice` and `_cut` stay within bounds.
  - The SSTORE2 init code and its read offsets are correct (ZetherVerifier.sol:143-161). The ERC-7201 slot matches `scaas.zether`.
  - The layer constructor checks that the verifiers share one psid, the same inner-product verifier and the right roles (ZetherLayer.sol:45-61).
- **Disclosure.** The SENDER_S check, the base and point choice per kind, `amount < 2^64`, `z < r` and `e ≠ 0` are correct. A HASH entry (49 + 40M bytes) can never be parsed as a stored record (16 + 512M bytes). The challenge preimage gap is C3-C2.
- **Gap round: admin overrides and kind features.**
  - `disableFeature` and `dropPending` cannot lock `mtxUnshieldUnwrap`. The wrap-disable and pending-rate checks sit only on the wrap side (UnderlyingLib.sol:140-141, 150, 207-208).
  - `executeRate` checks against `totalSupply()`, which includes SHIELD_POOL. A rate step between a wrap-shield and an unshield-unwrap keeps the reserve invariant.
  - CTR-ZTH-47 holds: AdminLib `_validFor` / `init` refuse FREEZE, FORCE, PAUSE and ALLOWLIST and the issuer / compliance roles for both Zether kinds. The admin mint and burn paths cannot touch the pools.
- **Gap round: CTR-ZTH-39 to 51.**
  - CTR-ZTH-39: there is no attest function; `verifyTransferAmount` is a view.
  - CTR-ZTH-41: a threshold auditor key is just a valid point, so no contract change is needed.
  - CTR-ZTH-45: the token has no register function.
  - CTR-ZTH-50: the memo is required exactly on HASH, has length 17 + 40M, carries the payload salt and is bound by memoHash, and the prover's seal_memo matches it.
  - CTR-ZTH-51: the public side of a UA shield and unshield is correct.
- **Gap round: customer-chain gas limits.** The EIP-7976 calldata floor does not bind at the admitted M. EIP-8037 state gas sits outside the 2^24 regular cap. A transfer that is too large reverts and nothing is lost. Roll-over and unshield are constant-size. The remaining gas items are C3-C5 and C3-C6.
- **Previous round (2026-10-19).**
  - W-B (verifier chosen by the creator) and V1-02 are closed by the implementation-immutable verifiers.
  - The W-I return-size checks are present, and the G1Bn254 returndatasize checks are now there too.
  - V1-07 (`verifyTransferAmount` reverts with `OpeningMalformed` instead of returning false, ZetherDisclosure.sol:46, 82) is still present and stays recorded.
