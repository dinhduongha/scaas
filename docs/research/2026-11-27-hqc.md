# HQC as SCaaS's second KEM family: status, cost and when (2026-11-27)

Research of 2026-11-27 for the owner, who asked for more on HQC after the PQ-only ruling for PqChat and PqVault
([CTR-UA-32](../contracts/accounts.md#CTR-UA-32), Change of 2026-11-27). Its sibling note
[2026-11-27-zklogin-chipmunk-kem.md](2026-11-27-zklogin-chipmunk-kem.md) §3 covers the SCaaS KEM as built and points here for
HQC. Nothing here is built, specified or ruled; the recommendation is a proposal. SCaaS's KEM code is **UNAUDITED**
([ARCH-POS-14](../decisions.md#ARCH-POS-14)). Gas figures marked *(estimate)* are computed from the per-byte prices below,
not measured.

## Summary

**Recommendation: after FIPS 207 is final, and then only for long-lived secrets, not for chat.** Do not build scheme 2 now: no
FIPS 207 draft is published (2026-11-27), and the HQC specification changed its parameters again on 2026-11-09 (v5.1), so
any code now would track a moving target. When FIPS 207 is final, consider HQC for PqVault epoch wraps (one wrap per member per
epoch) through a record that does not force every chat sender to pay it.

**Owner, 2026-11-27:** the cost is too high; HQC is reconsidered later, after FIPS 207 is final. Nothing is planned now.

| | ML-KEM-1024 + X25519 (scheme 1, today) | + HQC-1 | + HQC-3 | + HQC-5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HQC ciphertext | — | 4,433 B | 8,978 B | 14,421 B |
| one PqChat / PqVault wrap | 1,688 B | 6,121 B | 10,666 B | 16,109 B |
| wrap state gas (EIP-8037, SSTORE2) | 2.88 M (measured) | ≈ 9.66 M | ≈ 16.61 M | ≈ 24.94 M |
| wraps per 60 M block | ≈ 20 | ≈ 5 | ≈ 3 | **2** |
| `kemPk` record | 1,601 B | 3,842 B | 6,115 B | 8,838 B |

Main reason: an HQC-5 ciphertext is about nine times scheme 1's, and SCaaS keeps every ciphertext in contract state, so a
wrap's state gas rises from 2.88 M to about 25 M. **Cost of scheme 2:** the enabled scheme id and its length in every
registry, a combiner and vectors, an HQC implementation in `ua-rs`, `clientkeys` and every `ua-*`, and new gas ceilings for
PqChat and TreeKEM.

## 1. Standardisation status

- **NIST selection:** HQC was selected on 2025-03-11 as the fifth PQC algorithm and the second KEM, beside ML-KEM; NIST planned a
  draft standard "in about a year" and a final standard in 2027 [H1, H2].
- **FIPS 207:** NIST's presentation "FIPS 207: HQC-KEM" was given on 2025-09-25 (A. Robinson, Sixth PQC Standardization
  Conference) [H3]. **No FIPS 207 draft is published:** csrc.nist.gov's FIPS list shows FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (final,
  2024-08-13) and neither 206 nor 207, and the address `csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/207/ipd`, which the RustCrypto crate already
  cites, returns 404 [H4]. Secondary sources of October 2026 report the same [H5]. The pqc-forum thread "Update of HQC" [H6]
  carries NIST's remark that the name will be HQC-KEM; the forum could not be read in full on 2026-11-27 (connection reset).
- **Specification NIST is expected to take:** the HQC team's specification is now **v5.1 of 2026-11-09** [H7]:
  - v5.0 (2025-08-22) replaced the FO transform with a salted FO with implicit rejection after M.-J. O. Saarinen's 2025
    "IND-CCA2 issue in HQC" (many malformed ciphertexts shared one implicit-rejection value; liboqs's CVE-2025-48946 [H8]); it
    added `(ek, salt)` to the key derivation, aligned the hashes with FIPS 203 (SHA3-512 for the seeds, 32-byte `K`), made
    the fixed-weight sampler uniform by rejection sampling and added a seed-only decapsulation key.
  - v5.1 (2026-10-12, 2026-11-09) made the seed-only `dk` the default, changed the F_2^8 polynomial, and raised the weights
    ω (all levels) and ω_r (HQC-1, HQC-5) after a new decryption-failure analysis and Carrier, Hatey, Luzzi, Tillich, ePrint
    2026/1498 (minor information-set-decoding gains) [H7]. Sizes are unchanged; **v5.0 and v5.1 do not interoperate**
    *(inference: the parameters differ)*.
- **IETF** (all active Internet-Drafts, none adopted as an RFC) [H9]: `draft-preussmattsson-tls-hqckem-03` (2026-10-20, HQC-KEM
  for TLS 1.3), `draft-reddy-hpke-hqc-01` and `draft-reddy-cose-hpke-hqc-00` (2026-10-14), `draft-reddy-ipsecme-ikev2-hqc-00`
  (2026-10-24). No draft defines a hybrid of ML-KEM and HQC.

## 2. Security basis and why NIST picked it

HQC is a code-based IND-CCA2 KEM on the quasi-cyclic syndrome decoding problem (QCSD) over `F_2[X]/(X^n − 1)`, with a
concatenated Reed-Muller / Reed-Solomon decoder [H7]. It is a different family from ML-KEM's module lattices, which is the
point of a second KEM: if module-LWE falls, HQC does not fall with it.

NIST IR 8545 (March 2025) [H2]: among the fourth-round codes, NIST meant to standardise at most one of BIKE and HQC; both need
a low decryption-failure rate (DFR) for IND-CCA2, "NIST does not consider the DFR analysis for BIKE to be as mature as that for
HQC", and "HQC is not believed to require additional modifications". Classic McEliece was left to ISO (large keys, limited
interest). The HQC submitters named two patents; the owner committed to a free, worldwide, non-discriminatory licence for
implementing the standard [H2].

**Known attacks and implementation history:**

- Timing: Guo, Hlauschek, Johansson, Lahr, Nilsson, Schröder, "Don't reject this" (TCHES 2022): key recovery from the timing of
  rejection sampling seeded by secrets in decapsulation, about 866,000 idealised timing queries at level 1; fixed in v4.0 by
  the sampler of [H7]'s ref. 41 [H10]; the paper reports the leak also with the then constant-time BCH decoder.
- Design: the 2025 implicit-rejection issue above (fixed in v5.0) [H7, H8].
- Implementations: liboqs disabled HQC by default from 0.13.0 (2025) for CVE-2025-48946 and tracked a secret-dependent branch,
  CVE-2025-52473; it re-enabled `KEM_hqc_<1|3|5>` in 0.16.0 (2026-07-09) on the 2025-08-22 specification [H8, H11].
- Cryptanalysis: the v5.1 parameter rise answers ePrint 2026/1498's minor gains [H7].

The list shows that HQC's specification moved as recently as November 2026, two years after ML-KEM's became final.

## 4. Cost in SCaaS terms

### 4.1 Prices used

- **EIP-8037** (Last Call, deadline 2026-11-01) [H14], the pricing of SCaaS's tier-1 EVM chains as the docs use it
  ([evm.md](../networks/evm.md)): 1,530 state gas per byte (`CPSB`), so a code-deposited (SSTORE2) byte is 1,530 and a new
  storage slot 64 × 1,530 = 97,920; regular gas is capped at 2^24 by EIP-7825, state gas only by `tx.gas` and the block limit.
  Calldata floor 64 per byte (EIP-7976, as evm.md uses it).
- **Before EIP-8037** (chains without the fork): a new slot 20,000 + 2,100 cold; code deposit 200 per byte; calldata floor 40
  per non-zero byte (EIP-7623, Final [H15]).
- The **measured** scheme-1 wrap: a device key's wrap adds 108,944 regular and 2,876,400 state gas
  ([pqchat.md](../contracts/pqchat.md), CTR-CHAT-24 row). The HQC rows below add the HQC ciphertext's bytes to that measurement
  *(estimate)*.

### 4.2 One ciphertext, by storage form *(estimate)*

| Ciphertext | bytes | EIP-8037 SSTORE2 | EIP-8037 slots | before 8037, SSTORE2 (deposit + floor) | before 8037, slots |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| scheme 1 (ML-KEM-1024 + X25519) | 1,600 | 2.45 M | 4.90 M | 0.42 M | 1.11 M |
| HQC-1 | 4,433 | 6.78 M | 13.61 M | 1.10 M | 3.07 M |
| HQC-3 | 8,978 | 13.74 M | 27.52 M | 2.19 M | 6.21 M |
| HQC-5 | 14,421 | 22.06 M | 44.16 M | 3.49 M | 9.97 M |

SCaaS stores wraps in SSTORE2 blobs (CTR-CHAT-05), the cheaper column. Never lose ciphertext rules out keeping only a hash.

### 4.3 PqChat wrap and PqVault share box

A PqVault wrap is PqChat's (`member ‖ keyId ‖ mlkemCt ‖ x25519Eph ‖ sealedKey`, [pqvault.md](../contracts/pqvault.md)); a share
box is a shared vault per recipient set, so one wrap per recipient key per epoch ([CLI-PQV-02](../clients/pqvault-core.md#CLI-PQV-02)).
Per wrap with scheme 2 at HQC-5: ≈ 24.94 M state + ≈ 1.03 M regular, ≈ 27.3 M asked ((regular + state) × 1.05)
*(estimate)*; the summary table gives the other levels.

- **A 2-member session** (`createSession`, both wraps, ML-DSA-65, the default `sstore2` instance, measured 13.71 M state +
  0.56 M regular, [pqchat.md](../contracts/pqchat.md)): + 2 × 22.06 M state + 2 × 0.92 M regular ≈ 57.8 M state, 2.4 M
  regular, **≈ 63 M asked, over a 60 M block** *(estimate)*: the wraps would go by `addWraps` in their own transactions.
- **A share box of n recipients:** about n × 27 M asked, two wraps per 60 M block.
- **The 2^24 cap** binds the regular part only: an HQC-5 wrap's ≈ 1.03 M of calldata floor allows about 16 per transaction, so
  the block limit, through state gas, binds first.

### 4.4 TreeKEM at 512 keys

The 512-key ceiling ([pqchat.md](../contracts/pqchat.md), bounds: `TREE_LEAVES_MAX` 512, `TREE_CHANGES_MAX` 20) was measured
with 1,601-B node keys and 1,600-B path ciphertexts: the completion at its bounds 38.4 M asked, the node keys of depth 9 24.9 M,
the node keys as the last piece 52.1 M at 32 changes. With HQC-5 a node key grows 5.5 × (8,838 B, + 11.07 M state each) and a
path ciphertext 9 × (+ 22.06 M each) *(estimate)*: the node keys of one path alone pass 100 M, and at most two path
ciphertexts fit a block. **The 512 ceiling would not survive:** every piece would have to be re-split and re-measured, and the
owner's bounds (512, 20 changes) would likely have to fall. HQC does not fit TreeKEM on EVM.

### 4.5 PQZ opening legs

Each PQZ leg carries an `mlkemCt` per reader (recipient, and auditor where on) to the reader's opening KEM key
(the sealed opening, [CTR-PQZ-40](../contracts/pq-zero-knowledge.md#CTR-PQZ-40)), and a transfer has several legs (decoys
included). HQC-5 would add 22 M state per reader per leg *(estimate)*: a 4-leg transfer with an auditor would add about 176 M.
**Not feasible** on EVM, although the opening key is long-lived.

### 4.6 Non-EVM families, briefly *(estimate, at the family pages' prices)*

| Family | Price | HQC-5 ciphertext (14,421 B) | scheme 1 (1,600 B) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| NEAR | 10^19 yocto per byte ([near.md](../networks/near.md)) | 0.144 NEAR | 0.016 NEAR |
| Algorand | box MBR 400 µALGO per byte ([algorand.md](../networks/algorand.md)) | 5.77 ALGO | 0.64 ALGO |
| Solana | rent-exempt 5,080 lamports per byte ([solana.md](../networks/solana.md)) | 0.073 SOL | 0.008 SOL |

Solana's 1,232-B transaction and Algorand's argument limits mean an HQC ciphertext is written in chunks over several
transactions on those families; the other families' rent and size limits are each family round's to check.

## 6. What scheme 2 (ML-KEM-1024 + HQC + X25519) would need

[CTR-UA-32](../contracts/accounts.md#CTR-UA-32) reserves scheme `0x02` "after FIPS 207" and refuses it today, so no new
registry generation is needed. To enable it:

1. **The level.** The reservation names no HQC level. HQC-5 matches ML-KEM-1024's category; HQC-3 is about 0.6 of its size, HQC-1 about
   0.3. An owner call.
2. **The combiner.** Scheme 1's is X-Wing's shape over ML-KEM-1024, `SHA3-256(ss_M ‖ ss_X ‖ E ‖ pk_X ‖ "scaas.kem.1")`. A
   three-part combiner must bind the HQC part too; no published construction combines ML-KEM, HQC and X25519. The CFRG
   generic framework (`draft-irtf-cfrg-hybrid-kems`, companion of the concrete draft) is the place to take it from, and
   HQC's ciphertext binding has to be argued (or its ciphertext hashed in), since X-Wing's omission of the ML-KEM ciphertext
   rests on ML-KEM's own property *(inference)*.
3. **Ids and lengths:** `kemPk = 0x02 ‖ ek_M (1,568) ‖ ek_H ‖ pk_X (32)` and its exact length in every registry's check, and the
   `KemKey` struct gains a field (explicit parameters, CTR-UA-36).
4. **Vectors:** `vectors/ua/kem.json` rows for scheme 2, the FIPS 207 KATs once NIST publishes them, every client reading the
   same rows; the seed derivation of the HQC key from `kemSeed`.
5. **Clients:** `ua-rs` (`wallet::kem`), `clientkeys`, every `ua-*` binding, `pqchat-core`, `pqvault-core`; wasm32 builds.
6. **Contracts and node:** the registry's scheme check on every family; PqChat / PqVault wrap shapes and their gas pins; the
   node's job shapes.

**Worth its size?** As a UA-wide key, no: a scheme-2 key makes every sender to that UA pay about 25 M state gas per wrap,
chat included, and TreeKEM and PQZ legs do not fit (§4.4, §4.5). For long-lived secrets the trade differs: a PqVault epoch
key is wrapped once per member per epoch and protects data for years (harvest-now-decrypt-later is the threat a second
family hedges). The cheapest shape *(proposal)* is a separate, scoped KEM record for vaults, the way PQZ already has scoped
opening records, so a UA's chat key stays scheme 1 and only vault wraps pay HQC. That is a design change for the owner, not a
switch on CTR-UA-32.
