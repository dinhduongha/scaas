## Review Focus (inputs most likely to bite)

1. **Vacuous binding tests.** A mutated proof rejected before the trace point (z3 over `Bz3`, z4 over `Bz4`, h coefficient 0 or 32 non-zero) produces no seed record. "Missing record" must mean FAIL, never "seeds differ". Use kind 7 with `r1 & 12 == 0` and `c64 ∉ {0, 32}`, kind 5 at-bound (`(r1 >> 8)` even), and kind 14.
2. **Canonical form before encoding.** z3 / z4 centred with `redc` before grandom3, h in [0, q) with `redp` before urandom3, in all four implementations. Encode a reduced *copy*; never reduce in place an object used later (the generic verifier's `accumulate_z3` uses `z3`). In the SV, `F.z3` / `F.h` are not read after `lz_get`, so in place is acceptable there.
3. **The SV transcript buffer.** `buf` is allocated at `pqz_sv.c:1450` before the norm checks: size it from `proof_len` (F3), not `nty`.
4. **PoC harness patches must still apply.** `z34poc.sh` expects exactly 5 sed hits in `compute_z34` (D-3 lines 1635 / 1654 / 1675 are not among its patterns; keep it so). `l2dom/build.sh` patches the P6-L2 line. `sv/review/review.sh` patches `lnp-quad.c` with anchor `336,$`; D-2 adds lines before 336: re-check the `[ count = 1 ]` guards or move the anchors.
5. **Seeds that silently stay old.** In the SV, μ from `g2 = hash2` (`pqz_sv.c:1780`, `sv.rs:1628`) and the final round over `hash2` (`pqz_sv.c:1890`, `sv.rs:1723`). One left on `hash1` makes C SV and generic disagree; `sv/diff.sh` must catch it.
6. **Rng parity in `rej_onetime`.** One `u` draw always, C and Rust, also when the sign test rejects; otherwise proofs diverge and `oracle_prove` fails.
7. **Stale artefacts that still "pass".** The cheating fixtures, `fuzz/regress/valid/crash-b9881…` (a valid v2 input) and the stopped roll-out's calibration rows are v2: re-export, regenerate or discard each explicitly.
8. **Self-test hash.** `keccak(kat_digest ‖ verdict bytes)` (vectors README §Self-test), so it should stay `ed910a2c…d205`: verify, do not assume. If it changes, every pin moves (`SelfTest.cs`, `PqzSafety.sol` `SELFTEST`, `chain/e2e.py`, `script/pqz/e2e.py`, `selftest.json`, `expected-results.json`, docs).
9. **Allocation-failure paths in Rust.** New copies and the coder use `try_reserve` (`tests/memory.rs` denies allocations one by one); no panic reachable from input.
