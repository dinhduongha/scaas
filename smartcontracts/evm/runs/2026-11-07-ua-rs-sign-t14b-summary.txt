# Decisions (controller, the owner may override): (1) The parser lives in zether-prover (`state`, public data only: `prepare`
# with no key, then `run`), so ua-rs's facade and zether-read-balance share one parser; the facade calls `prepare` before it
# touches the key (key-use counter) and keeps its own allow-list over the same field lists. (2) Request shape: location
# `family` ("evm"; another family refused), `chainId`, `chainUid`, `token`, `block`, `psid`, `ua` (the reader, = the
# handle's); words `account` [the six acct[ua] slots], `accountData` [{slot, word}], `entryWords` [{slot, word}] (length slot
# first), `entryPtr`, `blob` {address, code}, `memoWords`, `modes` (slot 0), `auditorKeyWords`; an entry by `fromUA` +
# `sendCounter` (entryRef recomputed; every slot checked against it). (3) Debits keep an optional `balance`: the holder's
# own plaintext (its balance cache, zether-limits D5: a balance above the device BSGS bound is spent from the cache), checked
# against the rebuilt acc (`[b]G == C - [x]D`) before proving; without it the balance is BSGS of the rebuilt acc. sendCounter
# and accSeq come from the words; `disclosureMode` / `auditorMode` strings, `yTo`, `yAud`, legs, fee stay caller fields
# (public keys and policy the chain checks again). (4) openDisclosure decrypts the leg's amount inside (BSGS) and returns it with
# the opening; the auditor role needs `modes` auditorMode != NONE, the token's auditorKey = the handle's Y and a copy on the
# leg. (5) Every multiplication of a supplied point by x goes through `scaas_ct::secret_mul` (decrypt, check, the three debit
# balance checks, the receiver's / auditor's opening check), and so do the proofs' nonces over x on supplied points (A_bal of
# transfer, multi-recipient and burn; the opening's A2): their leak is x's. (6) zether-read-balance: `--rpc` with
# `--chain-id`, `--chain-uid` (genesis hash), `--factory`, `--token`, `--depth` or `--finalized` (R1-R5; every read by block
