## Surprises against the spike's numbers

- **M lands where §4 projected:** `NormalToken` 32,098 B (~32,160 [P]), `WrappedToken` 42,678 B (~42,470 [P]),
  `NormalZetherToken` 40,552 (~40,620), `WrappedZetherToken` 51,040 (~50,960), `WrappedPqzToken` 56,232 (~56,000). M is
  -8,755 B on `NormalToken` and -9,619 B on `WrappedToken` against today's base.
- **D yields less than projected on `WrappedToken`:** M + D 39,792 B against ~39,280 [P] (+512 B): `factory()` stays (the
  factory reads it on chain, `OpenFactory._checkImplementation`), and so do `hasRole`, `pendingOwner` and the views
  `ZetherStatements`, `ZetherDisclosure`, `SCaaSDex`, `OpenFactory` read; the spike's deletion took `factory`, `hasRole`,
  `pendingOwner`.
- **L is small and costly:** -1.9 to -2.1 KB per kind, and **+7.5 K gas** on every typed relayed transfer (§4 guessed +1 to
  +2 K, Zether's figure): a cold DELEGATECALL to the library, the calldata copied, `MtxPipelineLib` called from inside it, the
  return decoded. Its library is 9,296 B (15.79 M to deploy).
- **The EOA's relayed transfer costs +1.3 to +1.4 K** (spike: +875 to +992): R47's `UASig` on the generic entries does not
  touch `mtxTransfer`; the difference is R17's check and the base's own changes since the spike. EOA to a UA: +112 to +177
  (base `uaTransfer` from a padded EOA, the same calldata).
- **The signed cancel gets cheaper:** -3.1 to -5.1 K (Q2's body bumps the lane itself; the address form ran a self-call).
- **The router halves:** `GovernanceRouter` 20,073 -> 11,744 B (33.91 M -> 19.96 M asked). The 11,371 B on the D columns is
  an artefact (the router has no `fallback`; it loses the shared `mtxDigest` view): its true figure is 11,744 B.
- **The factory frame** `deployImplementation` adds: +0.36 to +0.37 M asked on top of the probe (included in the kinds'
  figures above). Asked per runtime byte: about 1,688 to 1,690 gas (spec §4: 1,685).
