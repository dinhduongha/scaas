== Jump width above 64 KiB
The assembler widens every jump target from PUSH2 to PUSH3 once code passes 65,535 B. Full mock:
WrappedPqzToken 82,726 B: 5,294 PUSH3, 471 PUSH2 => about 77,430 B at PUSH2 width
WrappedZetherToken 83,448 B: 5,151 PUSH3, 529 PUSH2 => about 78,300 B at PUSH2 width
So any figure above 65,536 B carries about 5.2 KB of width: what has to go is the PUSH2-width excess.
