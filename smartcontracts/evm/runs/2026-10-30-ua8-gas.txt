UA-8 Task A8: gas figures (forge 1.8.5, --isolate, evm_version amsterdam; deterministic).
Date 2026-10-30. Branch ua8. Tests: test/ua/UARegistryUA8.t.sol (test_T_GAS_register_ua8PerKind, test_T_GAS_storePqzPop,
test_T_GAS_setKemKey), test/ua/UAGas.t.sol, test/ua/UARegistryGroups.t.sol (test_T_GAS_groups).
Run per set:  scripts/capped smartcontracts/evm/script/pqz/at-set.sh <set> test --isolate --match-path 'test/ua/{UAGas,UARegistryGroups,UARegistryUA8}.t.sol' --match-test T_GAS
Re-pin:       GAS_REPIN=1 (same command, -vv) prints the GAS| rows without checking the pins.

Columns: regular gas (forge `gasTotalUsed`, includes the calldata floor), EIP-8037 state gas (`gasStateUsed`), sum.
Both are charged to the transaction's gas (gas used of `register` = regular + state; e.g. ed25519 314,604 + 1,164,330 = 1,478,934 =
the 1,479,483 "gas used" of UAGas.t.sol less ~0.5 k test overhead).

PINS ARE REGRESSION GUARDS ONLY: each constant in the tests is the first measured run, "/ 1.0"; none is a derived
bound. A change in the registry that moves a figure re-pins it deliberately (GAS_REPIN=1), it is not a limit.

NOTES
- mldsa65: the precompile (0x...5CAa0013) is an exact-input mock in forge, so the mldsa65 verify gas in every row below
  (register mldsa65*, setKemKey mldsa65, mldsa65 members of msig-pq*) is the mock's, not a real precompile's.
- 0x0142 (PQZ R_REG): the verifier is the `rreg` stand-in answering a mocked verifyPop, so the R_REG verify gas is not in
  the PQZ rows; the registry-side cost (calldata, hash, SSTORE2 of the key and the 28-35 KB proof, the log) is.
- Calldata floor: Amsterdam (EIP-7976) prices calldata at a floor of 16 gas per token (zero byte = 1 token, other byte = 4
  tokens) over the whole ABI calldata, i.e. 21,000 + 16 x tokens. storePqzPop(ua, proof) at n2304-kmax4096 has 30,500 B of
  calldata (selector, 3 head words, the 30,381 B proof padded to 30,400) of which 125 are zero: 21,000 + 16 x
  (4 x 30,500 - 3 x 125) = 1,967,000, which is the regular gas reported. The execution (hashing, SSTORE2 writes, and the
  243,048 gas of the proof as log data in UAPqzPopStored) sits under that floor, so the proof bytes in the event cost NO
  extra regular gas in storePqzPop; the no-op repeat call costs the same 1,967,000 (floor again).
- No gas price and no block limit is assumed by any figure or pin. Against a 60 M block (P2), regular + state of ONE transaction:
    * FULL registrations that exceed 60 M (register with pqzPop in the call, so they must use `register` without the proof
      and `storePqzPop` afterwards): the PQZ-carrying rows `mldsa65/<set>` and `msig-pq/<set>` at all four sets:
      84.7 M / 87.4 M (n2048-kmax1024), 91.5 M / 94.1 M (n2304-kmax4096), 98.2 M / 100.8 M (n2560-kmax16384),
      106.4 M / 109.1 M (n2816-kmax65536).
    * `register` without pqzPop, mldsa65/<set>: 39.2 M (n2048-kmax1024), 42.5 M (n2304-kmax4096), 45.7 M (n2560-kmax16384),
      49.0 M (n2816-kmax65536): fits. msig-pq/<set> (measured, Table 2): 42.0 M, 45.2 M, 48.5 M, 51.8 M: fits (n2816:
      8.2 M, 14 %, of a 60 M block left).
    * `storePqzPop`: 45.6 M, 49.1 M, 52.6 M, 57.5 M with the `rreg` stand-in, i.e. WITHOUT the real R_REG verify (the stand-in
      answers a mocked verifyPop): n2816-kmax65536 leaves only 2.5 M (4 %) of a 60 M block, the gen-2-over-gen-1 variant is
      57.7 M there. The real verify (~3.65 M) lifts the regular gas by ~2.4 M (the calldata floor 2.29 M already covers part of
      the execution), so n2816-kmax65536 is ~59.9 M WITH the real verify: right at a 60 M block, NOT "fits" (ruling A11-watch;
      watch item for fs3 Task 13, measure the real call before using n2816-kmax65536 on a 60 M-block chain). The other sets keep
      headroom (n2304-kmax4096 about 7 M).
    * Every other single-key / msig row fits (largest: slhdsa128s 14.4 M; mldsa44 11.3 M; mldsa65#1 9.7 M).
    * `setKemKey` of the 12 x slhdsa128s msig (96,832 B signature stored as the KEM record's `sig`): 158.3 M, over 60 M;
      this is a test construction (12-of-12 threshold) to get a >64 KiB registration blob, not a product row.
    * FROST(bn254) 16-of-16 (largest group; ruling A5-c3): `register` 1,259,582 regular + 28,816,020 state = 30,075,602:
      fits a 60 M block (50 %). MuSig2/FROST rows are in Table 4.
