Scenario (both sets): a NORMAL_PQZ token with bit 24 (OPTIONAL auditor), then a WRAPPED_PQZ token (kind 5) created under LOG.
d1 op 1 auditor on (NONE); a rollover; d2 op 1 auditor off after it (NONE); three shields in blocks of their own, two rolled;
carol (a receiver, a leg, then a sender) and the auditor rotated to version 1; setProofStore(LOG); d3 op 7 M = 4 with the
auditor, a rotated leg, at p = rolled - 1 (an un-roll); d4 burn 20; d5 refresh; d6 op 1 to the rotated receiver with the rotated
auditor; setProofStore(NONE); d7 the migration of carol from version 0; d8 an unwrap on kind 5 (LOG). Every debit has its own
ownTag. The reference rebuild (e2e.py `rebuild`, test code) reads only the §9 interface: pqzDebitRecord, pqzPendingCt, the op
blob (pqzEntryBlob, or the next same-version record's prevBlob / the account's accBlob for a burn) with its kv tail, pqzKeyAt by
version, the header from the PqzDebitProof log (LOG) or rebuilt from the calldata, record and token (NONE; and checked against
the logged header for every LOG debit), P from the log or the calldata; then pqz-ffi verify.

Negatives, one at a time, each must fail verification or the lookup: a wrong historical key version (d6 receiver), a swapped leg
(d3), a wrong auditor window (d6), a wrong shieldIn (d2, 0 -> 1), a wrong predecessor (d5 with d3's), a wrong Δp (d4), a
version boundary crossed (d7, the pending of version 1), P of another debit (d1 with d2's), a mode read from the wrong record
(d1, NONE, looked up as LOG: no log). Not covered here: a debit with shieldIn != 0 (P2 of T64 covers it), PQZH, OFFCHAIN, the
relayed paths (their binding is the envelope's), kind 13 (--skip-kind 13: its createToken reverts DeployFailed() on this copy).

Harness changes (e2e.py): section 7 and the rebuild; --proof-path-only; --skip-kind; EVM_OUT / PQZ_PROVE for another set; `at`
(a p below rolled) on transfer / transfer_multi / burn; burn returns its receipt; every proof kept (PROVEN); rpc() retries HTTP
429 and a refused connection. The runs send to the signer's RPC inside the copy's network: one 83,204 B transaction sent through
the follower never reached the signer (mined when sent to the signer directly), and the copy's nginx front answers 429 at its
10 r/s limit.
